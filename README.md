## Rebounce
Haven Gamejam

This is ReBounce, a fun fighting game about a slime against medieval armies.

## Controls
Press E to jump on enemies; doesn't work on blue shield enemies.

Press Q to dash and bounce back! Doesn't work on red shield enemies.

You aim where your dashes and jumps go with your mouse!

Enemies will attack to the rhythm of the song.
